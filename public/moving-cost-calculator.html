Moving Cost Calculator: estimate what your move will cost

MovingBuddy does not know local prices, so you type in the quotes or estimates you have. The calculator adds them up, works out fuel from distance, and shows where your money goes.

What to include

  • Moving company: the quoted price, including labor and insurance.
  • Rental vehicle: daily rate, mileage charges and any deposit you will not get back.
  • Packing materials: the Box Calculator gives you quantities to price up.
  • Storage: monthly cost multiplied by the months you need.
  • Additional services: cleaning, furniture assembly, disposal, parking permits.

How fuel is calculated

Fuel = one-way distance × 2 for the return journey × number of trips × consumption per 100 km ÷ 100 × fuel price. Trips come from the Truck Size Calculator when you have used it. If you hire a driver, fuel is normally in the quote, so set distance to zero.

This is a budgeting aid, not a quotation. Ask each supplier for a written quote and check what is excluded.

Worked example: a do-it-yourself move

Say you choose $ as the currency, enter $150 for a rental vehicle and $120 for packing materials, and set the distance to 50 km with the default consumption (12 L per 100 km) and fuel price (1.6 per liter). Fuel comes to $19.20 and the estimated total is $289.20: the vehicle is 52%, packing materials 41% and fuel 7%. These are illustration numbers, not real prices.

Entering US units

  • Distance: multiply miles by 1.609 to get kilometers.
  • Fuel consumption: divide 235.2 by your miles per gallon. A vehicle that gets 20 mpg uses about 11.8 L per 100 km.
  • Fuel price: divide the price per gallon by 3.785. A price of $4.00 per gallon is about $1.06 per liter.

Comparing quotes

  • Check whether the price is a flat rate or hourly, and what happens if the job runs long.
  • Ask what protection for your belongings is included and what it costs to increase it.
  • Ask about extra charges such as stairs, long carries, bulky items or parking.
  • Get quotes in writing so you can enter the same categories into the calculator.

Where the numbers come from

The Moving Box Calculator tells you how many boxes to price, and the Moving Truck Size Calculator gives the number of trips used for fuel. Schedule the bookings with the Moving Checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no default price?

Prices vary a lot by country, season and distance. Using your own numbers is more honest than guessing.

Does it include tips or taxes?

Only if you enter them. Use Other expenses for anything not covered.

Can I change the currency?

Yes, choose €, $ or £ at the top.

How much does it cost to move?

There is no single answer, because the cost depends on distance, the size of your load, the help you need and the time of year. This calculator has no built-in prices. Enter your own quotes and estimates to see your total.

How do I include a long-distance move?

Enter the one-way distance in kilometers. If you have used the Truck Size Calculator, its trip count is applied to fuel.
