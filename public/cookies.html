Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how MovingBuddy uses cookies and similar browser technologies when you visit our website.

2. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small files that websites can store in your browser. MovingBuddy currently uses browser localStorage for certain website features.

LocalStorage is different from cookies. It stores information in your browser and allows the website to remember certain information when you return.

3. What MovingBuddy stores

MovingBuddy currently uses the following localStorage keys:

  • mb_state: used to save information associated with your moving plan and tool preferences.
  • mb_notice: used to remember that you dismissed the browser storage notice.

This information is stored locally in your browser and is not sent to MovingBuddy's own application servers by these storage features.

4. Managing and deleting stored information

You can remove information stored by MovingBuddy by clearing this website's site data through your browser settings.

Please note that clearing this information may remove saved moving plans and preferences.

5. Third-party cookies and advertising

MovingBuddy does not currently use Google AdSense or intentionally set advertising cookies.

If advertising, analytics or other third-party services are introduced in the future, they may use cookies or similar technologies to provide their services.

Before introducing technologies that require consent, MovingBuddy will update this policy and implement an appropriate consent mechanism where required by law.

6. More information

For more information about how MovingBuddy handles personal information, please read our Privacy Policy.
