About MovingBuddy

MovingBuddy is an independent project created to make moving house easier to plan.

It provides a free set of practical tools for planning a house or apartment move, including box, volume, truck size, moving checklist and cost calculators that work together.

Our aim is simple: make your move easier to organize, understand and manage.

All results are estimates based on assumptions explained on each tool page. They are not quotations or professional advice.

MovingBuddy is currently independently operated. For questions or feedback, please visit our Contact page.
