Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

MovingBuddy respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit and use our website and how you can manage your information.

1. Who is responsible for this website?

MovingBuddy is an independently operated online project.

Data controller: Carles Viles Sánchez

Contact email: movingbuddy.contact@gmail.com

2. Information processed by MovingBuddy

MovingBuddy provides online moving-planning tools, including calculators and checklists.

Information you enter into these tools is stored locally in your browser so that your progress and preferences can be remembered when you return.

This information is stored on your device using browser storage (localStorage). The tool entries are not sent to MovingBuddy's own application servers.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing this website's data in your browser settings.

3. Contact messages

If you contact MovingBuddy by email, we will process the information you provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message, to respond to your enquiry.

Contact messages are received through our email provider. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

4. Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. When you visit the website, the hosting and delivery infrastructure may process technical information, such as IP addresses, device and browser information, and server request data, to deliver and protect the website.

Such processing is subject to the hosting provider's own privacy terms and applicable data protection requirements.

5. Cookies, advertising and analytics

MovingBuddy uses browser localStorage for certain tool preferences and saved information. LocalStorage is different from browser cookies.

At the time of this policy, MovingBuddy does not offer personalised advertising through Google AdSense and does not intentionally use third-party analytics tools.

If advertising, analytics or other third-party services are introduced in the future, this policy and the relevant consent mechanisms will be updated where required before those services are used.

6. Why and on what basis information is processed

Information may be processed to operate and protect the website, remember tool settings on your device, and respond to messages you send.

Depending on the activity, the applicable legal basis may include the steps needed to respond to your request, legitimate interests in maintaining a secure and functional website, or your consent where required.

7. Data retention

Information stored by the tools in your browser remains there until you delete the website's stored data or it is otherwise removed by your browser.

Emails are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry and any related responsibilities.

Technical information processed by the hosting provider is subject to that provider's applicable retention practices.

8. Your data protection rights

If applicable under data protection law, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to certain processing.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. This does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, contact us at: movingbuddy.contact@gmail.com .

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD): www.aepd.es .

9. Third-party services

Services used to host, deliver or operate this website may process technical information under their own privacy policies. Their processing is subject to their applicable terms and legal obligations.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, its services or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how MovingBuddy handles information, contact: movingbuddy.contact@gmail.com .
