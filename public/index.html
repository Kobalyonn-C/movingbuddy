One plan, five tools that share your numbers

Most moving calculators answer one question and forget it. MovingBuddy keeps your answers on your device so each tool builds on the last, and you never have to retype anything.

Where to start

Begin with the Moving Box Calculator if you are still packing, or the Moving Volume Calculator if you already know what is going on the truck. The Moving Truck Size Calculator then picks a vehicle, the Moving Cost Calculator adds up your budget, and the Moving Checklist schedules everything around your moving date.

Metric units

The calculators currently use meters, kilometers and liters. Each tool page includes the conversions you need to enter square feet, cubic feet, miles or gallons.

How MovingBuddy works

  1. Count what you have. Enter your home and belongings in the box and volume tools.
  2. Pick transport. The truck tool turns your volume into a vehicle and number of trips.
  3. Budget it. Add your quotes and see the total and breakdown.
  4. Follow the checklist. Set your moving date and tick off dated tasks.

Frequently asked questions

Is MovingBuddy really free?

Yes. All tools are free and need no account.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser using localStorage. MovingBuddy has no server database.

How accurate are the results?

They are estimates based on documented assumptions on each tool's page. Adjust the numbers to match your situation.

Do the tools work together?

Yes. Your box count feeds the volume calculator, volume feeds the truck calculator, and trips feed the cost calculator.

Do the calculators use metric units?

Yes. They use square meters, cubic meters, kilometers and liters. Each tool page explains how to convert from square feet, cubic feet, miles and gallons.
