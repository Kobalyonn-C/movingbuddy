Moving Checklist: a week-by-week plan for your move

Choose your moving date and get a checklist split into 8 weeks before, 4 weeks, 2 weeks, 1 week, moving day and after the move. Your progress is saved automatically in this browser.

Why plan in stages

Most moving stress comes from tasks that were left too late: a van that is fully booked, a utility transfer that takes ten working days, or a landlord notice period you forgot. Working backwards from your moving date puts each job in the window where it is easiest.

What each stage is for

  • 8 weeks: decisions and bookings, such as budget, mover, vehicle and decluttering.
  • 4 weeks: gathering supplies and giving notices.
  • 2 weeks: notifying utilities and changing your address.
  • 1 week: confirmations and the essentials box.
  • Moving day and after: handover, checks at the new home and settling in.

Your checks and custom tasks stay on this device only. Clearing your browser data removes them, so print the list if you want a copy.

How to use the checklist

  1. Pick your moving date. Each section then shows its due date, in month-day-year format, for example Dec 20, 2026.
  2. Tick tasks as you finish them. The progress bar and count update straight away.
  3. Add your own tasks, choose the section they belong to and press Add task.
  4. Press Print checklist for a paper copy.

The checklist starts with 24 built-in tasks, four in each of its six sections.

Tasks you may want to add

Everyone's move differs, so add what applies to you. Common examples are forwarding your mail with the postal service, updating your driver license and vehicle registration with your state, and transferring renters or home insurance.

Pair it with the calculators

At 8 weeks, set a budget with the Moving Cost Calculator and choose a vehicle with the Moving Truck Size Calculator. At 4 weeks, order supplies using the Moving Box Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically in your browser's localStorage. It is not sent to a server.

Can I add my own tasks?

Yes. Type a task, choose a section and press Add task.

What if I have less than 8 weeks?

Start with the closest stage and work through the earlier ones as quickly as you can. Moving company and utility tasks are the most urgent.

How do I print the checklist?

Press the Print checklist button. Buttons and navigation are hidden in the printed version.

Does it work for apartments and houses?

Yes. The tasks are general, so add anything specific to your home with the custom task form.
